1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: WHY RENTIDO WAS CREATED (THE PROBLEM & SOLUTION)
================================================================================

THE PROBLEM:
High-value creative, technical, and lifestyle gear—such as cinema cameras (RED Komodo,
Sony FX3), pro audio gear, commercial drones (DJI Mavic), heavy-duty GPU workstations,
and VR consoles—are prohibitively expensive to purchase outright (,000 to ,000+).
Meanwhile, hundreds of professionals and rental houses possess idle equipment sitting
on shelves losing value.

However, peer-to-peer equipment rental has traditionally failed due to 5 critical risks:
1. THEFT & FRAUD RISK: Owners fear handing high-value cameras to unvetted strangers.
2. DAMAGE & REPAIR DISPUTES: When equipment returns scratched or defective, renters
   claim 'it was already broken', and owners demand full replacement costs without proof.
3. SECURITY DEPOSIT FRICTION: Renters hate handing cash deposits to strangers who might
   never return the money or invent bogus deductions.
4. UNRELIABLE LOGISTICS: Handing off fragile, sensitive lenses and drones via ordinary
   courier services leads to broken equipment and zero custody tracking.
5. NO ACCOUNTING TRANSPARENCY: Payouts to owners are delayed, untracked, and prone to
   confusion regarding commissions and platform cuts.

THE RENTIDO SOLUTION:
Rentido is engineered as a **Trusted Local Equipment Rental Marketplace + Dual-Sided
Protection + On-Demand Logistics + Double-Entry Escrow Accounting**.

Rentido completely eliminates peer-to-peer risk through:
- 100% Vetted Asset Inspections: Mandatory technical condition audits before & after rental.
- Dual-Sided OTP Verification: Handover OTP and Return OTP prevent fake claims of delivery.
- Bank-Grade Double-Entry Escrow: The renter's money and security deposit are held in escrow
  and can only be released upon mutual signoff or audited administrative arbitration.
- Dynamic Surge & Pricing Engine: Fair prices based on peak demand, season, and coupons.
- Instant Owner Payouts: Settled revenue is disbursed on-demand directly into the owner's
  bank account or UPI, fully recorded in an immutable ledger.

3. COMPLETE ROLE BREAKDOWN: EXACTLY WHAT EACH USER ROLE CAN & CANNOT DO
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 1: RENTER / GENERAL CUSTOMER (e.g., ananya.renter@rentido.com)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
Filmmakers, creators, students, studio professionals, and gamers who need short-term
access to top-tier equipment without buying it.

WHAT THE RENTER CAN DO:
- Search & Discover: Browse live marketplace listings filtered by City, Category,
  Search Keywords (tokenized multi-word search), and Verified Condition Inspections.
- Detailed Price Breakdown: View exact base rent, dynamic surge multiplier, applied coupon
  discount, platform fee, delivery fee, and refundable security deposit before booking.
- Apply Promotional Coupons: Enter coupon codes (e.g. SAVE20, WELCOME50) for verified discounts.
- Book Gear with Escrow Protection: Pay rental fees + refundable security deposit.
- Dual-Sided Handover Verification: Receive a 6-digit Handover OTP to share with the
  delivery driver/owner only when the physical gear is inspected and accepted.
- Request Rental Extension: If a project runs long, request extra days directly through
  the platform at prorated rates.
- Return Verification: Confirm return through a 6-digit Return OTP.
- Auto-Refund of Security Deposit: Once return inspection passes with 0 damage, the
  held security deposit is automatically unlocked and refunded to their card/bank.
- Review & Trust Score: Rate the gear quality and owner reliability (1 to 5 stars).

WHAT THE RENTER CANNOT DO:
- Cannot view the Admin Command Deck or access Django Admin.
- Cannot view another owner's payout statements or financial ledgers.
- Cannot register physical assets or publish gear listings without activating an Owner profile.
- Cannot release their own security deposit without inspection clearance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 2: EQUIPMENT OWNER / GEAR PARTNER (e.g., rajesh.camera@rentido.com)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
Independent videographers, rental houses, drone pilots, and equipment collectors who
monetize idle high-value gear.

WHAT THE OWNER CAN DO:
- Access Owner Studio: Dedicated dashboard to manage equipment, listings, and earnings.
- Physical Asset Registration: Register gear with brand, model, serial number, replacement
  value, initial condition (Like New, Excellent, Good), and inspection criteria.
- Publish Marketplace Listings: Set daily rental rates, required security deposit, delivery
  options (Self-Pickup, Doorstep Delivery), and geographic coverage (city/area/pincode).
- Monitor Portfolio & Custody: Track which assets are Available, Rented, In-Transit,
  or Under Inspection.
- View Earnings & Escrow Ledger: Real-time visibility into credited earnings from completed
  rentals, active escrow balances, and transaction history.
- Request Instant Payout: Click 'Request Instant Payout' to disburse cleared earnings
  instantly into their bank or UPI account via double-entry financial ledger settlement.
- Dispute Damage Claims: If equipment returns damaged, initiate a formal dispute with
  inspection photos and repair quotes for Admin arbitration.

WHAT THE OWNER CANNOT DO:
- Cannot view or manage other owners' gear or private earnings.
- Cannot access platform-wide administrative controls or bypass inspection requirements.
- Cannot arbitrate their own disputes; disputes must be settled by an impartial Admin.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 3: PLATFORM ADMINISTRATOR / EXECUTIVE (e.g., admin@rentido.com)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
Rentido executive operations, finance, and legal officers managing the platform.

WHAT THE ADMIN CAN DO:
- Access Executive Command Deck: High-level overview of Gross Merchandise Value (GMV),
  platform revenue, commission cuts, active rentals, and held escrow funds.
- Escrow Ledger Audit: Real-time oversight into all double-entry ledger entries
  (ESCROW_INFLOW, OWNER_PAYABLE, PLATFORM_REVENUE, SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW).
- Direct Django Admin Access (/admin/):
  * Payments & Ledger: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/payments/ledgerentry/
  * Inspections: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/inspections/inspection/
  * Fleet Dispatches: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/logistics/deliveryorder/
  * Surge Price Rules: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/promotions/surgepricingrule/
  * Physical Assets: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin/assets/asset/
- Physical Asset Verification: Review newly listed equipment and approve or reject it.
- Dispute Arbitration: Settle damage disputes between owners and renters by allocating
  the held security deposit (e.g., award 3,500 damage compensation to Owner, refund
  the remaining 1,500 to Renter).
- Surge Pricing & Promotions: Create dynamic multiplier rules based on city, category,
  and festival seasons; configure promotional discount coupons and referral rewards.
- Tamper-Evident Audit Trail: Inspect read-only audit logs (/api/audit-logs/) recording
  who performed what action, when, and from what IP address.

WHAT THE ADMIN CANNOT DO:
- Cannot modify past financial ledger entries (all ledger entries are strictly immutable).
- Cannot delete audit trail records (enforced by database constraints).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 4: QUALITY & TECHNICAL INSPECTOR (e.g., priya.tech@rentido.com)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
Certified camera technicians, drone mechanics, and electronics specialists.

WHAT THE INSPECTOR CAN DO:
- Pre-Dispatch Technical Inspection: Before delivery, check optical alignment, sensor dust,
  shutter count, battery cycles, drone motor health, and mark inspection checklist.
- Post-Return Technical Inspection: Check returning gear for scratches, water damage,
  fungus, cracked glass, or missing accessories.
- Issue Quality Certifications: Certify gear as 'LIKE_NEW', 'EXCELLENT', or 'GOOD' to
  display verified badges on marketplace cards.
- File Damage Assessment Reports: If gear is returned defective, itemize repair costs
  and upload photographic evidence to feed directly into the Admin Dispute Arbitration engine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 5: LOGISTICS & FLEET DELIVERY DRIVER (e.g., arun.driver@rentido.com)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Who they are:
Trained logistics personnel handling specialized, shock-proof transport of fragile gear.

WHAT THE DRIVER CAN DO:
- View Assigned Dispatches: See scheduled pickup and delivery routes with asset details.
- Owner Pickup Verification: Collect gear from owner, verifying physical bag/case seals.
- Doorstep Handover via OTP: Deliver gear to renter and require the renter to provide the
  6-digit Handover OTP. Entering this OTP in the system officially transfers custody.
- Return Collection via OTP: Collect gear from renter at rental completion and verify Return OTP.
- Incident Reporting: Report transit delays, road accidents, or security seal tampering.

4. END-TO-END RENTAL LIFECYCLE: WHAT HAPPENS ON EVERY ACTION
================================================================================

Here is the exact step-by-step lifecycle of an equipment rental in Rentido:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: AUTHENTICATION & ROLE PROVISIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: User clicks 'Sign In / Register' in the top navbar and enters email & password.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Frontend calls POST /api/auth/login/.
2. Django verifies credentials and returns a SimpleJWT access token (7-day lifetime)
   and a refresh token (30-day lifetime).
3. Tokens are stored in browser localStorage.
4. User role profile (is_owner, is_admin, is_inspector, is_driver) determines visible
   nav buttons and authorized modals.
5. If the access token expires during a session, the frontend automatically calls
   POST /api/auth/token/refresh/ in the background without interrupting the user.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: ASSET REGISTRATION & LISTING PUBLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Owner opens 'Owner Studio', fills equipment specs, and clicks 'Publish Listing'.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Physical Asset Created: POST /api/assets/ records the hardware (Brand: Sony, Model: FX3,
   Serial: SN-FX3-9942, Replacement Value: 350000, Condition: LIKE_NEW).
2. Listing Created: POST /api/listings/ binds the asset to marketplace terms (Daily Rent: 2500,
   Security Deposit: 15000, City: Bangalore, Area: Indiranagar).
3. The listing is immediately published to the live marketplace catalog.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: CATALOG DISCOVERY, SEARCH & CATEGORY FILTERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Renter browses equipment cards on the homepage, clicks a category tile
(e.g., 'Cameras & Optics'), or searches 'Sony FX3'.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Category Filtering: The UI checks category slugs ('cameras-optics', 'drones-aerial',
   'laptops-workstations', 'audio-recording', 'lighting-grip', 'gaming-vr') against listing data.
2. Tokenized Multi-Word Matching: The search algorithm splits queries into words and matches
   all tokens against item titles and descriptions.
3. Smooth Auto-Scroll: The viewport smoothly navigates down to the #marketplace-listings grid.
4. Guaranteed Condition Badges: Listings display verified condition badges (Like New, Excellent)
   and owner trust scores.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: DYNAMIC PRICE COMPUTATION & COUPON VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Renter selects rental dates (e.g., 3 days) and enters coupon code 'SAVE20'.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Formula:
   Base Rent = Daily Rate * Days (e.g. 2500 * 3 = 7500)
   Surge Multiplier = Applied from active SurgePricingRule (e.g. 1.0x standard, 1.25x peak)
   Coupon Discount = Validated via POST /api/coupons/validate/ (e.g. 20% off base rent = -1500)
   Platform Fee = 10% commission on base rent (e.g. 600)
   Delivery Fee = 300 (or 0 for self-pickup)
   Security Deposit = 15000 (100% refundable escrow holding)
   Total Amount Paid = Rent + Fees + Deposit (e.g. 6000 + 600 + 300 + 15000 = 21900)
   Owner Payout = Rent - Platform Fee (e.g. 5400)
2. Live breakdown is rendered transparently on the rental drawer before checkout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: BOOKING & DOUBLE-ENTRY ESCROW LOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Renter confirms booking and clicks 'Pay & Lock Escrow'.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Payment Processed: Payment gateway (Razorpay / Stripe simulation) captures 21900.
2. Rental State: Rental created in status PENDING_CONFIRMATION or CONFIRMED.
3. SecurityDeposit Record Created: 15000 isolated with status HELD.
4. Double-Entry Ledger Created:
   - Debit: RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT 21900
   - Credit: ESCROW_INFLOW 21900
   - Debit: ESCROW_INFLOW 15000
   - Credit: SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW 15000
   Money is safely locked in the platform escrow vault. The owner cannot touch the funds yet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: PRE-DISPATCH INSPECTION & HANDOVER OTP VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Gear is packed, driver arrives at renter's location, renter verifies gear and gives OTP.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Pre-Rental Inspection: Technician confirms sensor is clean and lenses are pristine.
2. Handover OTP Generated: 6-digit cryptographic PIN generated on the rental.
3. Handover Execution: Driver enters OTP into POST /api/rentals/{id}/verify-handover-otp/.
4. Custody Transfer: Rental status officially becomes ACTIVE.
5. Inflow Crediting:
   - Debit: ESCROW_INFLOW 5400
   - Credit: OWNER_PAYABLE 5400 (Owner's earnings now credited as a pending liability!)
   - Debit: ESCROW_INFLOW 1500
   - Credit: PLATFORM_REVENUE 1500 (Rentido earns platform commission!)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: RENTAL IN-USE & RENTAL EXTENSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Renter uses the equipment. Renter realizes they need 2 additional shoot days.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Renter clicks 'Request Extension' via POST /api/rentals/{id}/extend/.
2. Platform calculates prorated rental for +2 days.
3. Renter authorizes additional payment.
4. Rental end_datetime is updated, and extra revenue is credited to owner payable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: RETURN COLLECTION & RETURN OTP VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Shoot finishes, driver picks up gear from renter, renter provides Return OTP.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Return OTP Generated: Renter views 6-digit Return OTP in their rental dashboard.
2. Driver enters OTP into POST /api/rentals/{id}/verify-return-otp/.
3. Custody Transfer: Rental status moves from ACTIVE to RETURNED. Gear moves to inspection facility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9: POST-RETURN INSPECTION & FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT SCENARIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Certified inspector checks the returned gear.

SCENARIO A: CLEAN RETURN (NO DAMAGE)
1. Inspector signs off checklist with condition 'EXCELLENT, NO DEFECTS'.
2. Security Deposit Status moves from HELD to REFUNDED.
3. Double-Entry Ledger Entry:
   - Debit: SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW 15000
   - Credit: RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT 15000 (100% of deposit returned to renter!)
4. Owner payable balance (5400) is officially cleared and ready for withdrawal.

SCENARIO B: DAMAGED RETURN (DISPUTE & ARBITRATION)
1. Inspector detects cracked front lens element, uploads photo, estimates 4000 repair cost.
2. Owner or Inspector files damage claim. Rental moves to DISPUTED.
3. Admin reviews evidence in Admin Command Deck under Arbitrate Dispute.
4. Admin executes POST /api/admin-ops/arbitrate-dispute/:
   - Deduct 4000 damage compensation to Owner.
   - Refund remaining 11000 to Renter.
5. Double-Entry Ledger Entry:
   - Debit: SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW 4000 -> Credit: OWNER_PAYABLE 4000
   - Debit: SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW 11000 -> Credit: RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT 11000
6. Full transparency, zero physical conflict between parties.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10: OWNER INSTANT PAYOUT & DOUBLE-ENTRY DISBURSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Action: Owner opens 'Owner Studio' -> 'Earnings & Payouts' -> clicks 'Request Instant Payout'.
Behind the Scenes:
1. Frontend calls POST /api/ledger/request-payout/.
2. Backend computes:
   Available Payout = Sum(OWNER_PAYABLE_CREDITED) - Sum(OWNER_PAYOUT_SETTLED)
3. If Available > 0:
   - Unique payout reference generated: PAYOUT-XXXXXXXX (e.g. PAYOUT-B615B605).
   - Double-Entry Ledger Entry created:
     * Debit: OWNER_PAYABLE 5400 (Liability cleared)
     * Credit: OWNER_BANK_ACCOUNT 5400 (Funds transferred to Owner UPI/Bank)
     * Rental: Linked to owner's completed rental.
   - Response returned:
     {'message': 'Successfully initiated payout of 5400.00', 'reference_id': 'PAYOUT-B615B605'}
4. Owner Studio displays green confirmation badge with payout reference ID.
5. Pending payout balance resets to 0.00 and appears in recent ledger statement.

5. HOW DOUBLE-ENTRY ESCROW ACCOUNTING WORKS (THE FINANCIAL ENGINE)
================================================================================

Rentido operates on strict GAAP-compliant double-entry ledger bookkeeping.
Every rupee moving into or out of the platform has a corresponding Debit and Credit.

THE 6 CORE LEDGER ACCOUNTS:
1. ESCROW_INFLOW: Temporary clearing account holding all inbound renter payments.
2. SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW: Segregated escrow account safeguarding renter deposits.
3. PLATFORM_REVENUE: Rentido's earned commission and service fees.
4. OWNER_PAYABLE: Platform liability representing cleared money owed to equipment owners.
5. RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT: External renter bank / debit / credit card account.
6. OWNER_BANK_ACCOUNT: External owner bank / UPI payout destination.

DOUBLE-ENTRY TRANSACTION FLOW CHART:

EVENT                      DEBIT ACCOUNT                 CREDIT ACCOUNT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Booking Payment         RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT           ESCROW_INFLOW
2. Deposit Quarantine      ESCROW_INFLOW                 SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW
3. Handover Confirmed      ESCROW_INFLOW                 OWNER_PAYABLE (Owner Revenue)
                           ESCROW_INFLOW                 PLATFORM_REVENUE (Commission)
4. Clean Return Settlement SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW       RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT (Full Refund)
5. Dispute Damage Payout   SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW       OWNER_PAYABLE (Damage Fee)
                           SECURITY_DEPOSIT_ESCROW       RENTER_BANK_ACCOUNT (Remainder)
6. Owner Instant Payout    OWNER_PAYABLE                 OWNER_BANK_ACCOUNT (Disbursed)

WHY THIS MATTERS:
Money can never 'disappear' or be double-spent. An admin or auditor can review the
LedgerEntry table at any moment to verify that every single rupee is accounted for.

6. GLOSSARY OF CORE CONCEPTS & SYSTEM TERMS
================================================================================

- Escrow: A secure financial arrangement where a trusted third party (Rentido's engine)
  holds funds until contractual conditions (handover, clean return, inspection) are met.
- Double-Entry Ledger: An accounting method where every financial transaction records
  both a debit and a credit, ensuring total assets always match total liabilities.
- Security Deposit: A refundable deposit required to protect against damage, loss, or late
  return, held safely in escrow during the rental period.
- Handover OTP: A 6-digit one-time PIN used by the renter to acknowledge receipt of the
  gear in good working order from the owner or delivery partner.
- Return OTP: A 6-digit one-time PIN used by the delivery driver or owner to confirm
  the physical retrieval of the equipment from the renter.
- Surge Pricing Rule: An automated rule that adjusts equipment daily rental rates by a
  percentage multiplier (e.g. 1.2x) during peak demand, festivals, or weekend rushes.
- Vetted Asset: Physical equipment that has undergone serial number registration, replacement
  value verification, and a certified technical inspection checklist.
- Trust Score: A dynamic rating (0 to 100) calculated from verified ID, on-time return
  rate, equipment condition maintenance, and positive community reviews.
- Dispute Arbitration: The administrative workflow where an Admin examines post-return
  inspection reports and fairly splits the held security deposit between owner and renter.
